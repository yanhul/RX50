1. It is the single most referenced evidence source in the repository (appears in C-01, C-02, C-03, C-10, C-11, C-20b, C-22, OI-15, T-G4-06).

4. Unlocking the IINJ value is required for T-G4-06 pass/fail.
5. Unlocking Table 6 is required to close OI-15.
6. It does not require any other evidence, owner decision, or RX24 artifact to obtain.
7. It does not require a measurement.

**Action:** Procure DS5319 Rev 20 (or latest revision) from STMicroelectronics and store in `evidence/`.
