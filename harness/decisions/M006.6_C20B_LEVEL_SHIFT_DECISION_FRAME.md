## 1. Problem Statement

The RX50 continuity measurement circuit uses a CD4067 analog multiplexer to route external signals to the STM32F103 ADC. Two supply voltage configurations are under consideration:

- **STM32 supply:** 3.3 V
- **CD4067 supply:** either 3.3 V or 5 V (source rail not yet selected)

If the CD4067 is operated at 5 V, a logic-level incompatibility may exist between the STM32 GPIO outputs (which drive the CD4067 address lines at 3.3 V logic levels) and the CD4067 digital input thresholds at 5 V.

Additionally, if the CD4067 is operated at 5 V, the 5 V-referenced sense node (the common I/O of the CD4067) may exceed the STM32 ADC pin's absolute maximum VIN when selected.

### 2.2 CD4067 VIH (from C-20b)

| Value | Historical claim |
|---|---|
| VIH at 5 V supply | 3.5 V |

**Status:** **HISTORICAL CONCLUSION — UNDERLYING EVIDENCE ABSENT**
(SCHS052D not in repository; VIH varies with VDD)

### 2.3 Implication if both historical values are correct

If VOH(STM32 @3.3V) = 2.9 V and VIH(CD4067 @5V) = 3.5 V, then:

> VOH (2.9 V) < VIH (3.5 V)

This means a STM32 GPIO output at logic-high (2.9 V) would be below the CD4067 input threshold (3.5 V), and the CD4067 may not recognize the logic-high state. This would cause incorrect channel selection.

**However:** Both values are historical conclusions from absent datasheets. The level incompatibility may be smaller or larger than the 0.6 V gap stated here. It may not exist at all if the CD4067 is operated at 3.3 V instead of 5 V.

## 5. Technical Options

Options listed below are identified from the existing repository context (C-20b, T-G4-05, T-G4-06). Other options may exist.

### Option A — Level-Shift the STM32 GPIO to 5 V Logic

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| What it does | Insert a 3.3 V → 5 V level translator (e.g., 74HCT4050, discrete FET, or dedicated level shifter) between STM32 GPIO outputs and CD4067 address inputs |
| What must be verified | (a) Level-shifter availability and cost; (b) propagation delay vs scan timing; (c) impact on address timing for C-21 |
| Evidence needed | DS5319 (VOH), SCHS052D (VIH), level-shifter datasheet |
| Design consequences | Additional PCB area, cost, propagation delay. Does not resolve the 5V→ADC sense exposure issue. |

### Option B — Operate CD4067 at 3.3 V Supply

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| What it does | Power the CD4067 from the same 3.3 V rail as the STM32 |
| What must be verified | (a) CD4067 is guaranteed to operate at 3.3 V (per SCHS052D operating range); (b) VIH at 3.3 V is compatible with STM32 VOH; (c) leakage at 3.3 V is within acceptable limits; (d) RON at 3.3 V is acceptable for the continuity measurement; (e) 5 V signals can be handled or are not needed |
| Evidence needed | SCHS052D (operating range, VIH @3.3V, RON @3.3V, leakage @3.3V) |
| Design consequences | Eliminates level incompatibility. Eliminates 5V→ADC sense exposure (since sense node is at 3.3 V). May reduce signal range for continuity measurement. Changes the continuity circuit architecture. |

### Option C — Replace CD4067 with a 3.3 V-Compatible Analog Multiplexer

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| What it does | Select a different mux (e.g., 74HC4067 or a lower-voltage analog mux) that is guaranteed to operate at 3.3 V with logic inputs compatible with STM32 output levels |
| What must be verified | (a) Availability of pin-compatible 3.3 V mux; (b) leakage at 3.3 V; (c) RON; (d) channel count; (e) cost and lead time |
| Evidence needed | Alternate mux datasheet, comparison to SCHS052D |
| Design consequences | A different component in the BOM. Requires re-verification of all mux-related specifications (leakage, RON, bandwidth). Eliminates level incompatibility if the chosen mux has 3.3 V-compatible logic inputs. |

### Option D — Operate CD4067 at 5 V with Level-Shift and 5V→ADC Mitigation

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| What it does | Use level-shifting for address lines (Option A) AND add sense-node attenuation (voltage divider) or buffering to protect the ADC pin from 5 V. This is the combined version of the two separate mitigations described in C-20b. |
| What must be verified | Everything from Option A plus: (a) voltage divider accuracy vs continuity measurement; (b) clamp diode leakage; (c) impact on measurement resolution |
| Evidence needed | DS5319, SCHS052D, level-shifter and protection circuit datasheets |
| Design consequences | Most complex option. Requires both level-shifting and ADC protection. May be necessary if continuity measurements must be performed at 5 V signal levels. |

## 6. Separate Issue — 5 V → ADC Sense-Node Exposure

Independently of the logic-level decision, C-20b identifies a safety issue:

> "5 V-referenced node read on ADC pin ≤4.0 V VIN, EV-48, no sense-node attenuation defined"

If the sense node operates at 5 V (regardless of whether CD4067 control logic is level-shifted), the ADC pin voltage may exceed the STM32 VIN absolute maximum (historically 4.0 V, unverified pending OI-15).

**Two mitigations proposed in C-20b (owner must decide):**

| Mitigation | Description |
|---|---|
| (a) Sense-node division/clamp ≤4.0 V | Add a voltage divider or clamp diode between CD4067 common I/O and STM32 ADC pin |
| (b) Restrict 5 V node reads to channel side | Only connect the 5 V node to the mux channel input, not to the common I/O; the ADC reading is always at 3.3 V-referenced level |

**Required before:** 5 V runs of T-G4-05 (leakage characterization) and T-G4-06 (current limit test).

**This issue is NOT solved by this document.** It is preserved for owner decision.
