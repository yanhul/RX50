## Purpose

Provide an auditable, symbolic timing framework for the RX50 continuity ADC scan, preserving explicit labels for every unverified historical value. No numerical result in this document is a verified RX50 performance prediction.

## A — ADC Conversion Time

### Formula (from historical context)

> tCONV = tS + 12.5 ADC clock cycles

**Source:** C-02, resolution of C-02 (per DS5319 Table 47)

**Status:** **HISTORICAL CONCLUSION — UNDERLYING EVIDENCE ABSENT**
(DS5319 Table 47 not present in repository)

### ADC clock period

> fADC = 14 MHz  →  tADC_CLK = 1 / 14 MHz = 71.43 ns

**Source:** C-10 (per DS5319 Table 48)

**Status:** **HISTORICAL CONCLUSION — UNDERLYING EVIDENCE ABSENT**
(DS5319 Table 48 not present in repository)

### tCONV per channel (with 55.5 cycle tS)

> tCONV = (tS + 12.5) × tADC_CLK
> tCONV = (55.5 + 12.5) × 71.43 ns
> tCONV = 68 × 71.43 ns
> tCONV = 4.857 µs

**Historical floor value (from C-02 resolution):** 64 µs / N channels — not independently recalculated here because the channel count N is unknown and the 4.857 µs per-channel value depends on unverified tS and fADC assumptions.

## G — Total Scan Time (Symbolic Formula)

> t_SCAN = N × (t_CONV + t_SETTLE + t_FW) / M

Where:
- `N` = total channels per scan (UNKNOWN)
- `t_CONV` = tS + 12.5 cycles at fADC (HISTORICAL — DS5319 absent)
- `t_SETTLE` = SR propagation + MUX transition + MUX settling (ASSUMED)
- `t_FW` = firmware overhead per channel (ASSUMED)
- `M` = number of independent ADCs available (HISTORICAL — 2, but architecture not confirmed)

### Historical floor values (from C-01, C-02)

These are the scan-time lower bounds recorded in the contradiction register. They are **not verified results** — they are the outcome of the M002 correction. They omit t_SETTLE and t_FW entirely.

| Configuration | Historical floor | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ADC, all N = 63 channels sequential | 64 µs per scan? (from C-02: "A=64 µs (1 ADC)") | **HISTORICAL CONCLUSION — not independently verifiable** |

| Option B configuration | 50 µs per scan (from C-02: "B=50 µs") | **HISTORICAL CONCLUSION — not independently verifiable** |

**Important:** These floor values were derived by M002 using N=63/2 ≈ 32 channels per ADC (if split), or another specific N, and omitting select/settle/software (C-02 explicitly: "lower bounds, omitting select/settle/software").
